Our Story

Slow Smoked Perfection Since Day One

We are a Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business bringing authentic Southern BBQ to Wildwood, The Villages, and Oxford, Florida. Our pitmasters slow-smoke certified meats for up to 14 hours over real hickory logs with our secret family spice rub.

From tender fall-off-the-bone ribs and melt-in-your-mouth brisket to our famous hand-pulled pork and jumbo smoked wings, we do barbecue the traditional way — with time, patience, and love.
