Who We Are

Born from Passion, Built on Veteran Pride

M&M Old Hickory Smokehouse BBQ was founded by Mark and Teresa, both proud U.S. military veterans. Located in Wildwood, Florida — right down the road from the famous 75 Chrome Shop — our smokehouse was born from an unwavering love of authentic, slow-smoked Southern barbecue.

After serving our nation, we brought that same discipline, integrity, and relentless pursuit of excellence to the pit. We believe there are simply no shortcuts in real barbecue. No gas burners, no electric cabinets, and no boiling. We burn 100% aged split hickory wood day in and day out, tending fires by hand from the earliest hours before sunrise.

Every cut of meat is hand-trimmed, seasoned with our trade-secret spice blend, and smoked low and slow until it achieves that tell-tale mahogany bark and ruby-red smoke ring. It is why our neighbors across Wildwood, The Villages, Oxford, and Sumter County have made us their go-to smokehouse.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

Real Hickory Wood

We burn only authentic seasoned hickory and oak hardwood logs. The smoke flavor is rich, deep, and clean — exactly the way true Southern BBQ has been cooked for generations.
